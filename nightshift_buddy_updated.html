About NightShift Buddy
💡 Why it exists
Night shift workers face unique challenges: fatigue, isolation, and mental health struggles. NightShift Buddy was built to provide immediate, compassionate support right at your fingertips.
🎯 Core features
Fatigue tracking, AI mental wellness support, handoff management, and smart scheduling tools — all designed with healthcare workers in mind.
❤️ Our mission
To reduce fatigue-related errors, prevent burnout, and foster a culture of mental wellness in healthcare. You deserve support. You are not alone.
